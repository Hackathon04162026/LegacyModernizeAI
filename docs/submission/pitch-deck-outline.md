# LegacyModernizeAI Pitch Deck Outline

Use this outline as a PDF-ready deck for judges, sponsors, and enterprise stakeholders. Keep each slide visual, with one message per slide and a short spoken takeaway.

## 1. Title
- `LegacyModernizeAI`
- `Modernization planning workspace for legacy applications`
- One-line value statement: turns legacy codebases into clear modernization plans, risk insights, docs, and target-state samples

## 2. The Enterprise Problem
- New engineers spend weeks just understanding old systems
- Legacy upgrades are slow because the real stack is often hidden across code, libraries, and databases
- Knowledge lives in a few senior people, not in documentation
- Security, PII exposure, and technical debt make modernization risky
- Teams need a faster way to decide what can move, what needs review, and what should wait

## 3. What LegacyModernizeAI Does
- Scans a repository and identifies technologies, libraries, and database patterns
- Produces a plain-language modernization plan
- Highlights security, complexity, and PII risk areas
- Generates developer-ready documentation for handoff and onboarding
- Creates a target-state sample to show what the upgraded experience could look like

## 4. Home
- Show the clean entry point for the workspace
- Repository path or sample folder selection
- Quick analysis button to begin the scan
- Simple progress feedback so users know the system is working
- No clutter, no deep details yet, just the starting point

## 5. Overview
- Give the current-state snapshot
- Show detected stack, databases, and library footprint
- Show modernization readiness in a way business leaders can understand
- Highlight how much manual effort can be reduced with AI-assisted analysis
- Frame the app as a decision-support tool, not just a scanner

## 6. Analysis
- Surface the high-risk areas that slow migrations
- Show security issues that need attention before upgrade work begins
- Show complexity hotspots that can delay delivery
- Flag PII exposure and other enterprise concerns
- Recommend what can be automated and what needs human review

## 7. Documentation and Confluence
- Turn scan results into onboarding-friendly documentation
- Capture what the application does, what changed, and why it matters
- Show a Confluence-ready output for team handoff
- Reduce tribal knowledge and shorten ramp-up time for new developers
- Keep the documentation practical, readable, and easy to maintain

## 8. Roadmap
- Show a phased modernization path instead of a big-bang rewrite
- Separate quick wins from deeper refactoring work
- Estimate effort for manual-only work versus AI-assisted work
- Help teams plan delivery with realistic sequencing
- Communicate risk, scope, and impact clearly

## 9. Sample Generation
- Create a target-state sample from the selected technology choices
- Show what the future version could look like
- Give stakeholders something concrete to react to
- Reduce ambiguity before a real migration starts
- Turn the plan into a visual proof point

## 10. CI/CD Value
- Show how the workflow fits into real enterprise delivery
- Support repeatable scans on new commits or pull requests
- Keep modernization visibility inside the delivery process
- Make the platform feel operational, not just experimental
- Demonstrate that modernization can be part of everyday engineering work

## 11. Why It Matters
- Faster onboarding for new engineers
- Less dependency on a handful of experts
- Lower migration risk
- Better visibility into security and maintainability
- Clearer decisions on what to modernize first

## 12. Closing
- LegacyModernizeAI helps teams move from uncertainty to action
- It turns a hard-to-read legacy system into a modernization plan, documentation, and a roadmap
- It gives enterprises speed, clarity, and confidence
